Turn-based PuzzleScript grid puzzles; mechanics vary. Each episode is one level (possibly late-game) with no title, rules, or win condition—only the grid and its responses. Moves: `up`/`down`/`left`/`right`/`action` (`undo`/`restart` exist); solutions ~5–12 moves. Grid: cells split by `|`, fixed slots per cell, letters (per-game, arbitrary) or `.`; legend lists present objects, including decorative ones. Avatar moves one cell, blocked by walls; usually pushing; wins typically "every X on a Y". Other mechanics discovered by play; rules can chain; unwinnable states arise silently.
